# Buick E5 T61 · Dock and functional-surface separation

Date: 2026-10-25. Base T60 `43d3127a3a264ce43c2dab19eacbce8766ef0234`.

## Real-device feedback and scope
The user reported that the **left Dock** still looked like a hard full-width
rectangle and the currently running app icons were oversized. They clarified:
**keep the original background crisp (no frosted-blur effect)** and distinguish
the menu rail from app/function content.

T61 changes:
- Landscape E5 left Dock: crisp wallpaper, no RenderEffect blur; inset rounded
  translucent menu rail with clear wallpaper visible outside the pill.
- Time, Wi-Fi, cellular status and battery remain genuine readings.
- Side Dock glyphs shrink from 30dp to 24dp while keeping approximately
  42dp hit targets (padding increased from 6dp to 9dp).
- Home keeps the original tappable size, with a restrained capsule.
- Fullscreen third-party app and fullscreen map surfaces are **not** clipped
  into little rounded windows: APP_CORNER_RADIUS=0f. Only real dashboard map
  tile keeps obvious 32px card corner radius.
- Default wallpaper blur preference becomes zero for fresh settings. Existing
  blur sliders and saved values still exist for eligible other displays;
  the E5 landscape Dock is intentionally always sharp.

Out of scope: virtual display size, split-panel hit testing, CarPlay transport,
audio/video channels, map navigation data, Shizuku and HID mapping.

**T61 is pending real-car acceptance.** Check app switches, Dock Home,
actual battery/network display, visual background continuity and map split/fullscreen.
No source photos are committed or linked here.
